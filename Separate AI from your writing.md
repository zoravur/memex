The problem with mixing AI with your own thoughts is you can confuse how well you've thought through an idea.

It also doesn't capture the unique idiosyncrasies that make your writing valuable in the first place.

Also, it's worth mentioning that unless you're still in school and desperate to hit a word count, there's no real reason not to just write up the points that you _do_ have - the imputing of the argument can be handled by AI anyway. 

This approach reduces [[Informational Entropy|informational entropy]].
